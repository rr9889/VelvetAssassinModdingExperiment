# Failure modes

SESSION EVIDENCE is attributed to observable records or explicit user reports. **User-reported only** means the archival request names the incident but original diagnostics/fix transcripts were not recovered. Proposed causes/remedies are GENERALIZED INFERENCE; no successful repair is invented.

| Failure / evidence | SYMPTOM | CAUSE | FIX | HOW TO VERIFY | REUSABLE LESSON |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
